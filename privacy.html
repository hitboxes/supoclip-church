Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

SupoClip Church ("the app") is operated by the media team of Joyful Church of Philadelphia ("we"). It runs on a computer the church operates and is used only by our media team to make YouTube Shorts from our own sermons and publish them to the church's YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

When a team member connects the church's YouTube channel, the app asks Google for two permissions through the YouTube API Services:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the Shorts our team makes to the connected channel, with the title, description and visibility the team sets.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the connected channel's name and ID, and view, like and comment counts for videos the app uploaded, so the team can see how the Shorts perform.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • Only to upload the church's Shorts and to show our team how they perform.
  • We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.
  • The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How it is stored

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted in the app's database on the church computer.
  • Channel details and video statistics are stored in the same database and are used only inside the app.
  • Disconnecting the channel in the app revokes the app's access and removes its tokens.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time, either by disconnecting the channel in the app or from your Google Account's third-party connections page. To ask us to delete stored data, contact us at the address below.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
