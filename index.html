SupoClip Church

Sermon Shorts for Joyful Church of Philadelphia · 필라행복한교회 설교 쇼츠

SupoClip Church is a private tool used by the media team of Joyful Church of Philadelphia to turn our Sunday sermons into short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) and publish them to the church's own YouTube channel.

필라행복한교회 미디어팀이 주일 설교 영상을 짧은 세로 영상(쇼츠)으로 만들어 교회 유튜브 채널에 올리기 위해 사용하는 내부 도구입니다.

What it does

  • Prepares each new Sunday sermon from the church's YouTube channel so clips can be made quickly.
  • Cuts the moments our team chooses into vertical Shorts with captions.
  • Uploads those Shorts to the church's YouTube channel on a schedule our team sets.

It is not a public service. Only the church's media team uses it, on a computer the church operates.

Privacy

How this tool uses Google and YouTube data is described in our Privacy Policy, and the rules for using it in our Terms of Service.
